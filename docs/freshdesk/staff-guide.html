For teachers, tutors and the education and membership team. It explains what Membership Bridge does to your courses, and what to do when something looks wrong. You don't need to change any settings. Site administrators set the plugin up (see the Membership Bridge: admin setup and use article).

What Membership Bridge does

Membership Bridge links Moodle to iMIS, our membership system:

  • It enrols people automatically. When someone registers for an event in iMIS, they are enrolled in the matching Moodle course. If needed, a Moodle account is created for them.
  • It removes access when a registration is cancelled. Their enrolment is suspended, not deleted, so their grades and progress are kept.
  • It sends results back to iMIS. Course completions and quiz scores (and other activity results, if your site sends them) go to the member's record in iMIS.
  • It can build tutorial groups. Groups set up in iMIS appear as groups in the course.

iMIS is the source of truth. Make registration and group changes in iMIS, and Moodle follows.

Checking a learner's enrolment

  1. Open the course and go to Participants.
  2. Find the learner. Check the Status column:
    • Active: they have access.
    • Suspended: their registration was cancelled, or they were removed. Their grades and progress are still there.
    • Not listed: they are not enrolled. See A learner can't get into the course below.
  3. Enrolments made by Membership Bridge show the method Manual enrolments.

Tutorial groups

  • Synced groups sit in a grouping called Tutorial groups (synced). On sites set up before version 2.2.1 the grouping may be called Tutorials (iMIS).
  • Tutors marked in iMIS are given the tutor role in the course, and enrolled if they weren't already.
  • Change groups in iMIS, not in Moodle. If you remove someone from a synced group in Moodle, the next overnight sync puts them back.
  • Your own groups are safe. Groups you create yourself in Moodle are never changed, and people you add by hand to a synced group are left there.

Please don't

  • Don't unenrol learners by hand. Unenrolling can remove their grades and completion. Cancel the registration in iMIS instead; Moodle suspends the enrolment and keeps the record. If you must remove access in a hurry, suspend the enrolment: on Participants, click the edit (cog) icon next to the learner's enrolment and set Status to Suspended.
  • Don't change a learner's username or ID number. That field links them to iMIS. If it is wrong, ask a site administrator.
  • Don't rename or delete synced tutorial groups in Moodle.

Common situations

A learner can't get into the course

  1. Check the registration in iMIS: it should be for the right event and not cancelled.
  2. Ask the learner to log out of Moodle and log back in. This checks their registrations within a few minutes.
  3. Check Participants again.
  4. If they are still missing, send a site administrator the details listed under Asking for help below.

A learner has re-registered after cancelling

Nothing to do. When the new registration is active, their suspended enrolment is reactivated overnight (or at their next login), with their previous grades and progress.

A learner's completion or score isn't showing in iMIS

  1. Check the learner has actually completed the course, or that the grade appears in the course Grades page. A mark on an attempt only counts once it reaches the gradebook.
  2. Allow a few minutes, then check iMIS again.
  3. If it is still missing after an hour, ask a site administrator. They can see every result sent to iMIS and whether iMIS accepted it.

A learner appears twice, or the wrong person was enrolled

This usually means two Moodle accounts share a member ID. Don't fix it yourself: ask a site administrator, giving both account names.

Asking for help

Contact a site administrator, and include:

  • the learner's name and member ID;
  • the course name (or the course link);
  • the event code from iMIS, if you have it;
  • what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and roughly when.
